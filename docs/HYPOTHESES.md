# Hypotheses

## H-PERSIST
Experiential information may leave persistent neural representations beyond ordinary conscious accessibility.

### Null
No experience-specific information above chance remains measurable after the relevant memory is inaccessible to conscious recall.

## H-ACCESS
Some forgetting may reflect impaired access to retained information rather than total erasure.

## H-REINSTATE
Previously experienced dream-state information can sometimes be reinstated in later sleep.

## H-CONTINUITY
Dreams judged as recurring or continuing contain objective semantic, spatial, narrative, or sensory structure shared with earlier records beyond chance.

## H-LUCID
Lucid dreaming can provide timestampable intentional internal actions useful for neural decoding.

## H-DECODE
Measured neural signals contain enough information to discriminate predefined internal experiential states above chance.

## H-RECONSTRUCT
Given sufficiently informative neural representations, decoded latent information can support progressively richer multimodal reconstruction.
