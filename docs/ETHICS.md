# Research Governance

## Public repository
Do not commit personally identifying raw dream reports, neural recordings, health records, credentials, tokens, or participant identifiers.

## Human participants
Research involving other people should use informed consent, appropriate institutional/ethics review, privacy and retention plans, and explicit withdrawal procedures.

## Intervention boundary
Invasive devices, surgery, pharmaceuticals, ionizing radiation, or neuromodulation are outside bootstrap scope and require qualified medical/institutional oversight.

Initial Morpheus work is observational, computational, and non-invasive.
