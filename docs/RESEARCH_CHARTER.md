# Morpheus Research Charter

## Long-range question
How much information about subjective human experience remains physically recoverable from the brain after ordinary conscious access has changed or disappeared?

## Working thesis
Morpheus investigates whether experiences leave persistent neural information and whether forgetting can sometimes reflect access failure rather than complete information loss.

This is a hypothesis, not an established fact.

## Initial domain
Dreams are the first domain because they combine visual, spatial, auditory, somatosensory, emotional, mnemonic, narrative, and occasionally deliberate lucid experience.

## Operating rule
Radical hypotheses are allowed. Unfalsifiable claims are not.

Every major hypothesis must define:
- a null
- measurable variables
- falsification criteria
- provenance
- versioned protocols
- retained negative results
