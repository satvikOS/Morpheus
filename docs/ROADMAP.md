# Roadmap

## M0 — Dataset Zero
Immutable raw dream capture, hashing, structured annotations, recurrence links, provenance.

## M1 — Recurrence
Text/graph similarity, world clustering, sequence alignment, prospective recurrence evaluation.

## M2 — Reinstatement
Controlled awakenings, interruption-duration experiments, pre-registered continuation criteria.

## M3 — Neural decoding baseline
Public EEG/fMRI datasets, reproducible preprocessing, held-out decoding.

## M4 — Live physiology
LSL discovery, synchronized markers, high-density EEG compatibility, XDF recording, quality metrics.

## M5 — Individual neural atlas
Subject-specific representation learning, perception/imagery calibration, sleep-state alignment, cross-session stability.
